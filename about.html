What Sets Us Apart

Four Real Reasons Northeast Ohio Chooses Choice Aire

In an industry flooded with private-equity consolidators and aggressive commission salespeople, here is why our approach is fundamentally different.

⚙️

1. In-House Custom Sheet Metal Shop

Most HVAC contractors buy generic, flimsy cardboard-backed transitions or flexible duct that restricts airflow and creates noisy static turbulence. At our 1940 Englewood Ave. facility, our tinsmiths shear, brake, and bend heavy-gauge galvanized steel to fit your home's exact measurements, guaranteeing aerodynamic laminar airflow and maximum equipment lifespan.

🚫

2. Zero High-Pressure Commission Quotas

Our service technicians are career tradesmen paid hourly for honest diagnostic accuracy—not commission percentages on how much equipment they condemn. If a $25 capacitor or flame sensor will safely keep your 12-year-old furnace running, that is exactly what we will recommend.

🏬

3. Dual Residential & Commercial Mastery

Unlike contractors who only touch residential split systems, Choice Aire also engineers complex 50-ton commercial rooftop units, restaurant beverage cooling lines, walk-in chillers, and industrial dust collection. This deep commercial-grade engineering expertise elevates every residential home installation we perform.

🛡️

4. The White-Glove Property Protection Pledge

We treat your home with total reverence. Our technicians put on clean medical shoe covers before stepping through your doorway, lay heavy padded neoprene runners along all transit hallways, and vacuum your basement utility area spotless before packing up.
