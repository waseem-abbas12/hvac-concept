Homeowner Guide

Repair vs. Replace: How to Make the Smart Choice

Unsure whether you should invest in fixing your current unit or upgrade to a modern high-efficiency system? Here is our honest, zero-pressure rule of thumb.

Factor When to Repair Your System When Replacement is More Economical
System Age Under 10 years old with healthy compressor/heat exchanger. 12 to 18+ years old approaching end of service life.
The $5,000 Rule Repair cost × system age is less than $5,000. Repair cost × system age exceeds $5,000.
Refrigerant Type Modern R-410A system with minor seal or valve repair. Phased-out R-22 Freon system with active coil leak.
Utility Bills Bills are stable and within normal seasonal range. Electric or gas bills have spiked 30–50% due to loss of efficiency.
Comfort Balance Temperatures remain consistent room-to-room after repair. Chronic hot/cold spots, persistent humidity, and short-cycling.
