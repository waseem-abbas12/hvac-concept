What Happens During a Choice Aire 26-Point Tune-Up?

Our N.A.T.E. certified master technicians don't just change a filter. We test, calibrate, sanitize, and safety-verify every component.

⚡

Electrical & Motor Diagnostics

  • Test start/run capacitor microfarad ratings
  • Inspect contactor points for pitting & arcing
  • Measure compressor running amperage vs. RLA
  • Measure blower motor amp draw vs. FLA
  • Tighten all high-voltage wire connections
  • Inspect and test high/low safety limit switches
  • Test thermostat calibration and heat anticipator
  • Inspect disconnect switch and service wiring
🔥

Combustion & Safety Verification

  • Fiberoptic heat exchanger camera inspection
  • Calibrated flue gas carbon monoxide (CO) test
  • Clean and microamp-test furnace flame sensor
  • Inspect and clean hot-surface electronic ignitor
  • Measure gas manifold pressure with digital manometer
  • Verify draft inducer motor operation & pressure switch
  • Inspect flue exhaust venting for rust or blockage
  • Test emergency gas shut-off valve function
❄️

Cooling, Coil & Airflow Audit

  • Wash outdoor condenser coil fins to restore heat transfer
  • Flush and clear condensate drain line & trap with algaecide
  • Inspect indoor evaporator coil for biological slime
  • Measure supply vs. return Delta-T temperature drop
  • Measure static pressure drop across air filter cabinet
  • Inspect outdoor fan blade balance and motor bearings
  • Check refrigerant subcooling & superheat levels
  • Inspect duct insulation and basement return plenums
