The Smartest Way to Heat an Ohio Home: Dual-Fuel Hybrid

Why choose between gas and electric when you can have the financial and comfort benefits of both automatically?

How Dual-Fuel Works:

A Dual-Fuel system pairs an electric heat pump on the outside of your home with a gas furnace on the inside. A smart communicating thermostat continuously tracks outdoor temperatures and local utility rates.

Moderate Winter Days (35°F to 60°F): The electric heat pump runs at hyper-efficient 300%+ COP efficiency, providing gentle, continuous warmth at a fraction of natural gas costs.
Deep Ohio Freezes (Below 30°F): The smart system seamlessly ignites your gas furnace for blazing, robust heat when gas combustion is most economical and responsive.
Summer Cooling: The exact same heat pump operates in reverse, acting as an ultra-high efficiency variable-speed air conditioner with precision humidity removal.
