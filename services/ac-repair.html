Common Warning Signs

Symptoms Your Air Conditioner Needs Professional Service

Don't ignore minor cooling issues until the compressor fails completely. Call us if you notice any of these signs.

Blowing Lukewarm or Warm Air

Typically caused by low refrigerant levels, a frozen evaporator coil, or a dead compressor start capacitor.

Grinding, Squealing, or Buzzing

Loose blower belts, failing bearings, or electrical contactor arcing require immediate attention before total motor burnout.

Water Leaking Around the Indoor Unit

Clogged condensate drain lines can overflow into your basement or ceilings, posing a significant water damage threat.
