Energy Math

What Does AFUE Really Mean for Your Monthly Gas Bill?

Annual Fuel Utilization Efficiency (AFUE) measures how many cents of every gas dollar actually heat your home versus getting wasted up the chimney.

65% – 70%

Your 15–20 Year Old Furnace

For every \$1.00 you spend on natural gas, 30 to 35 cents is instantly lost through drafty metal flues. Heavy temperature swings between cycles.

80% AFUE

Standard Entry Replacement

Uses traditional B-vent metal chimney. A reliable choice for replacement situations where running new PVC direct-venting pipes through finished basement ceilings is impossible.

96% – 98% AFUE

Bryant Evolution Condensing System

Only 2 to 4 cents lost. Secondary stainless steel heat exchanger extracts hidden heat from combustion vapor. Saves up to \$500+ every winter in Northeast Ohio.
