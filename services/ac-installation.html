Replacement Assessment

Should You Repair or Replace Your Air Conditioner?

HVAC systems rarely fail without warning. If your outdoor condenser or indoor coil exhibits two or more of these signs, replacement will save you thousands over continual breakdown calls.

⏳

System Age Over 12–15 Years

Even well-maintained units lose efficiency over time. Systems installed before 2012 typically operate at 10 SEER or lower, using double the electricity of modern 16–20+ SEER2 units.

🚫

Obsolete R-22 Freon® Refrigerant

The EPA completely banned R-22 production. If your older unit springs a leak, recharging it costs hundreds to thousands. Modern R-410A and next-gen low-GWP refrigerants are standard.

📈

The "$5,000 Rule" Threshold

Multiply the age of your equipment by the repair estimate. If a 12-year-old unit needs an $800 repair ($12 \times 800 = $9,600 > $5,000), investing in a new warranty-backed unit is the smarter financial choice.

🌡️

Hot Upstairs & High Humidity

Single-stage legacy systems short-cycle, dropping air temperature quickly without running long enough to remove muggy Ohio summer humidity. Modern two-stage or inverter units solve this permanently.
