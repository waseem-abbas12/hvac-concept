The Four Pillars of Pure Indoor Air

Room air purifiers only clean a single corner. Our duct-integrated solutions purify every cubic foot of air circulating through every room in your home simultaneously.

🛡️

1. Whole-Home Media Air Cleaners

Standard 1-inch fiberglass furnace filters protect the blower motor, not your lungs. We install deep 4-to-5-inch high-efficiency MERV 11 to MERV 16 media cabinets with custom sheet metal transitions in your return air drop. Requires replacement only once per year.

  • Traps 99% of pollen, ragweed, and dust mite debris
  • Eliminates persistent cooking odors and pet dander
  • Low static pressure drop keeps airflow strong and silent
💧

2. Whole-Home Steam & Flow Humidifiers

When Ohio heating kicks on in November, indoor relative humidity drops below 15%—drier than the Sahara Desert. Choice Aire installs automated bypass and electrode steam humidifiers that deliver precise 35%–45% humidity across every room.

  • Prevents sore throats, itchy skin, and respiratory irritation
  • Protects hardwood flooring, antique wood furniture, and pianos
  • Moist air feels 3° warmer, allowing you to lower thermostat setpoints
☀️

3. Germicidal UV-C Light Systems

Your AC evaporator coil is dark, damp, and cool—the perfect breeding ground for mold and bio-film slime. Ultraviolet lamps bathe the coil in 254nm germicidal light, neutralizing mold and microbial organisms before they enter your airstream.

  • Sterilizes evaporator coils and condensate drain pans
  • Eliminates 'dirty sock syndrome' odors in summer
  • Improves heat transfer efficiency across cooling coils
🍃

4. Energy Recovery Ventilators (ERVs)

Modern tightly insulated homes trap stale air, carbon dioxide, and VOC off-gassing indoors. An ERV expels stale interior air while drawing in fresh outdoor air, transferring thermal energy and humidity between streams so you don't waste furnace heat or AC cooling.

  • Continuous supply of fresh outdoor oxygen
  • Flushes out lingering chemical vapors and radon trace gases
  • Recovers up to 80% of heat or cooling energy from outgoing air
